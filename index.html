01.1

Support Engineer Role

The training begins with the support organization, the engineer career path, and the role of support as a first layer for customer issues.

DAY 1
ROLE FOCUS

Technical Support Engineer

The role sits at the intersection of technical investigationReviewing the issue, evidence, environment and relevant technical context to understand what is happening., customer communication, and product knowledge. The training frames support as more than closing cases: the goal is to help customers succeed while building strong technical expertise.

01 Customer success

Support work is tied to customer outcomes and service excellence.

02 Product expertise

Engineers are expected to keep developing product knowledge and technical proficiency.

03 Continuous growth

The program introduces clear career paths and continued learning as part of the role.

CAREER CONNECT

A role with multiple growth paths

The Day 1 material presents Technical Support Engineer as a starting point that can grow into senior and principal technical support paths, as well as technical account-oriented paths.
