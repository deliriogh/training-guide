03

Salesforce Data Model

Objects, fields and configuration concepts that define how a Salesforce org is structured.

Account ⓘRepresents an individual account such as a customer, competitor or partner.
Contact ⓘRepresents a person associated with an account.
Lead ⓘRepresents a prospect or lead.
Opportunity ⓘRepresents a sale or pending deal.
Custom Object ⓘA custom object used to model business information beyond the standard objects.
Record Type ⓘTraining material identifies Record Type as a Salesforce configuration concept.
